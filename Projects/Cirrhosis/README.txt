The target group for this project was Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA), which is the group in charge of dealing with organ transplants in the United States. By looking to create a model that can evaluate and quantify individual need for transplants, their job of deciding which lives to prioritize can be simplified. And seeing that there is over a hundred thousand transplants performed each year in the United States alone, a small percent improvement can lead to a massive saving of time. The model does this by not only classifying a patient's status of life versus death, but also by applying a likelihood of death, not just at a single point, but over time as well.
